# Heat-Up of Reheat Furnace 4 After Refractory Repair

**Document:** OP-HT-006 **Revision:** 3 **Effective:** 2026-01-14
**Applies to:** Heat treatment shop, furnaces 1-6
**Related:** EN 746-2 (firing safety), MSR-11 Furnace burner management, PTW-02 Confined space

---

## 1. Before you start

1. Confirm the refractory contractor's completion certificate for the repaired
   area, and that the work permit is closed and the scaffolding is out.
2. Confirm no tools, timber or shuttering left inside the chamber. Walk the
   peeking ports with a torch.
3. The lining was re-gunned eleven days ago. The cure schedule in section 3 is
   mandatory until the lining moisture is below 1%.

## 2. Prove the furnace safe to fire

4. Open the flue damper to the purge position.
5. Start the combustion air fan and purge the chamber for **not less than
   fifteen minutes** with the damper open to atmosphere.
6. Carry out the gas valve proven test on the main burner train, per MSR-11
   section 4: isolate, pressurise to 100 mbar, observe the decay for two
   minutes. Record the result on the heat-up sheet.
7. Confirm the flame supervision UV scanner on burner 1 is clear and proven.
8. Confirm the shell temperature survey points on the repaired roof are
   reachable and that the pyrometer battery is good.

## 3. Light-off and cure

9. Light the pilot on burner 1 and confirm the flame on the scanner.
10. Admit main gas at the minimum firing position. If the burner does not light
    on the first attempt, lock off, re-purge for fifteen minutes.
11. Set the ramp rate to **60 °C/h** on controller TRC-4C and confirm the
    rate shown on the controller face matches the rate you entered.
12. Hold the ramp at 60 °C/h until the zone reaches 300 °C. Watch the lining
    moisture readout; above 1% the rate is not to be increased whatever the
    mill asks for.
13. Record the shell temperature at the four survey points every thirty minutes.
    A reading over 260 °C is a hot spot: stop the ramp and call the shop
    engineer. Do not cover a hot spot by increasing the air.
14. Above 300 °C the rate may be increased to 150 °C/h to reach the operating
    temperature of 1150 °C.

## 4. Hand over

15. Notify the mill that furnace 4 is at temperature and record the finish time,
    the cure curve and your initials on the heat-up sheet.
16. Return the ramp rate to the normal production setting.

## 5. If something goes wrong

- If you hear a bang or see the roof lift, stop firing at the local emergency
  stop, close the main gas valve, and evacuate the front of the furnace. Do not
  open a peeking port.
- If the zone temperature does not respond to a rate change, check you are at
  the right controller before changing anything else.
