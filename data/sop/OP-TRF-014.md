# Transfer of Product from Road Tanker to Storage Tank TK-101

**Document:** OP-TRF-014 **Revision:** 7 **Effective:** 2026-03-02
**Applies to:** Bulk terminal, loading rack 3, tanks TK-101 to TK-104
**Approved by:** Operations Manager
**Related:** PTW-02 Hot work, ISN-05 Instrument isolations, API RP 2350 overfill protection policy

---

## 1. Purpose

This procedure covers the transfer of a flammable liquid product from a road
tanker into an atmospheric storage tank through the terminal transfer pump.
It assumes the tank is available to receive and that the overfill protection
functions are in service.

## 2. Before you start

1. Hold a valid transfer permit for the tank named on the dispatch note.
2. Check the tank status board. If the tank has had instrument work in the last
   14 days, obtain the isolation register from the control room and confirm
   that the level transmitter LT-101 and the high-high switch LSHH-101 have
   been returned to service and signed back by the instrument fitter.
3. Confirm that the vehicle is bonded and earthed and that the hose is made up
   with the correct couplings and the break-away arm in service.

## 3. Line-up

4. Open the tank inlet valve **V1** on TK-101.
5. Confirm the tank draw-off valve **V2** is closed.
6. Confirm the cross-connect valve **V3** to TK-102 is closed.
7. Walk the line from the tanker to the tank and confirm every valve is in the
   position stated above.
8. Confirm the tank vent valve **V4** is open. The vent line was isolated for
   the roof inspection work; if in doubt, ask the control room to confirm on
   the roof camera.
9. Open the hose box, check the hose for damage and confirm the drip tray is
   clear.

## 4. Starting the transfer

10. Start pump **P-201** and confirm flow is heard at the tank inlet.
11. Record the start level from the local indicator on TK-101 on the permit.
12. The transfer takes approximately 30 minutes. Go back to the rack and watch
    the level indicator. Stop the pump when the level reaches **9.30 m**.
13. If the level is not rising as fast as you expect, check the line-up again
    before you increase the pump rate. Do not open the recirculation bypass to
    keep the pump loaded.

## 5. Finishing

14. Stop pump **P-201** when the meter on the rack reads **55 m³**.
15. Close the tank inlet valve **V1**. Stop and isolate the pump at the local
    handle.
16. Drain and disconnect the hose into the drip tray. Return the hose to the
    hose box and hang the "not in service" tag on the vehicle stand.
17. Enter the delivered quantity, the finish level and your initials on the
    tank log. Update the tank status board.

## 6. If something goes wrong

- If product is released on the rack deck, stop the pump immediately from the
  rack emergency stop, close V1 if you can reach it safely, and call the
  control room on channel 1.
- If the level keeps rising after you have stopped the pump, the tank is
  receiving from somewhere else. Call the control room. Do not assume the
  pump stopped the flow.
